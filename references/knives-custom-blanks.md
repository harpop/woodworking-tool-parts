# Custom knives (Custom ξυράφια), "Blanks for profiling"

Source: TIGRA Woodworking Catalog 2023, **pages 44–51** (PDF pages 24–27), column **T04F-CR** only. We work with every T04F-CR item on these pages: 222 sizes.

## What they are and how we use them

- These are carbide **blanks** (ακατέργαστα πλακίδια). We grind the profile (προφίλ) into the cutting edge on profile-grinding machines (e.g. WALDER). The ground knife then cuts that profile into the wood.
- This is the difference from standard knives (`knives-standard-reversible.md`), which are mounted **as supplied** with a straight edge.
- **The user designs the profile.** How to draw the profile isn't covered yet.
- **Selection:** the required profile (the shape of the finished wood) decides which blank to use. The blank has to be large enough to contain the profile:
  - **L** has to cover the profile width along the cutting edge.
  - **W** has to leave room for the profile depth, measured from the profile edge towards the holes, plus the material needed around the fixing holes.
- Only the T04F-CR codes listed below may be used. Never pick a blank outside these tables.

## Seat edge vs profile edge (έδρα / προφίλ)

- The **long edge nearest the hole(s)** is the **seat (έδρα)**. That edge rests on the seat of the tool body, and **e** is measured from it.
- The **profile is ground on the opposite long edge**, on the far side of the holes.
- The seat edge comes in three forms:
  - **Flat (ίσια):** a square edge.
  - **35° bevel (τροχισμένη 35°).**
  - **Two small chamfers (2 μικρά chamfer):** Italian Style only, in place of a flat seat.
- Italian Style (pp. 46 & 51) seats are either bevelled 35° or chamfered. They are never plain flat.

## Italian Style rule (verified on all 66 Italian codes)

- Every Italian Style blank has **d = 4.5** and **e = 6.5**.
- Non-Italian blanks vary:
  - d is 3.6 / 4.1 / 4.2 / 4.5 / 5.0 / 5.5 / 6.0, mostly 4.1.
  - e is 4.75 / 5.5 / 6.0 / 6.3 / 6.5.
- Exception to watch for: **035291(80x25.4x2)** on p50 is not Italian Style, but it also has d = 4.5 / e = 6.5.

## Dimensions (from the catalog sketches)

- **L** is the length along the cutting edge.
- **W** is the width.
- **T** is the thickness: 2.0 mm (2.5 mm on a few p48 sizes).
- **d** is the hole diameter. It is mostly 4.1 mm; some sizes use 3.6 / 4.2 / 4.5 / 5.0 / 5.5 / 6.0. The Italian Style blanks are always 4.5.
- **e** is the distance from the hole centre to the **seat edge**: 4.75 / 5.5 / 6.0 / 6.3 / 6.5 mm.
- **a** is the distance between hole centres (2-hole blanks only). The holes are symmetric about the knife centre.
- 1-hole blanks have the hole centred along L.
- p48 is the only family whose side-view sketch shows the 35° bevel on **both** long edges, the seat and the profile side.
