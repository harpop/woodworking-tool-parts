# Standard reversible knives (Standard ξυράφια)

Source: TIGRA Woodworking Catalog 2023, **page 24** (PDF page 14, left half), first table, column **T04F-CR**.

## How we use them

Standard knives are mounted on the tool **as supplied**, with their shape unchanged and a straight cutting edge. This is the difference from custom knives ("Blanks for profiling"), which we grind to a profile.

## Geometry

- A rectangular carbide knife (πλακίδιο), **reversible**: both long edges are cutting edges. When one edge wears out, the knife is turned 180°.
- **Bevel / wedge angle (γωνία λοξοτομής): 35°** on the cutting edges.
- Dimensions:
  - **L** is the length along the cutting edge.
  - **W** is the width.
  - **T** is the thickness.
- Fixing holes:
  - **Short knives (L ≤ 25, no "a")** have **1 hole**, centred.
  - **Longer knives** have **2 holes**. **a** is the distance between hole centres, and the holes are symmetric about the knife centre.
- **Hole diameter d = 4.1 mm** (all knives in this table; value from the user, since the catalog table omits it).
